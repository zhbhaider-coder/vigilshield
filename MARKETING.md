## Product name
**VigilShield — PC Guard**

## Tagline
A watchful guard for your Windows PC: it monitors, blocks known threats, and alerts you the second something happens.

## Description (Gumroad long text)

**Your PC is under attack every day. Most people never notice.**

VigilShield sits quietly on your Windows computer and watches —
your network connections, new programs starting up, and your files.
The moment it spots a connection to a known attacker server, it
**blocks it automatically** and shows you a big red warning telling
you exactly what it stopped and when.

### What VigilShield actually does

- **Real-time network watch** — every outbound connection is checked
  against a threat list of known malicious servers, updated from
  public threat-intelligence feeds (abuse.ch, FireHOL).
- **Automatic blocking** — on a match, the attacking connection is
  killed and the server is blocked in your Windows Firewall.
  One click undoes every block it ever made.
- **Instant red alerts** — a full, unmissable warning with sound the
  moment a threat is stopped. No silent failures.
- **Suspicious program detection** — warns you about shady programs
  starting from temp folders with disguised names.
- **Ransomware tripwires** — hidden canary files in your Documents;
  if something starts mass-encrypting your files, you get warned.
- **Exposure scan** — a plain-language report of where your PC is
  exposed *right now* (firewall off? risky ports open? protection
  disabled?) so you can fix it before attackers come.
- **Quarantine, not deletion** — suspicious files are moved aside,
  never deleted. Nothing is irreversible.
- **NEW in v2 — Guardian mascot interface**: the cyber-girl Guardian
  sits at the center of the dashboard. Blue = protected, red = she
  just blocked a threat, pulsing cyan = scanning.
- **NEW in v2 — live threat map**: blocked attacker servers appear as
  markers on a world map (positions approximate), plus an attack
  timeline of your recent security events.
- **NEW in v2 — USB guard**: warns the second a USB drive is plugged
  in and offers to scan it for the classic USB malware tricks.
- **NEW in v2 — startup guard**: lists programs that start with
  Windows, flags suspicious ones, one-click disable (backup saved).
- **NEW in v2 — hosts-file guard**: detects hijacks of your hosts
  file, shows what changed, one-click restore.
- **NEW in v2 — quarantine manager + whitelist**: restore or delete
  quarantined files; one click to trust an IP or program forever.
- **NEW in v2 — one-click installer**: Start Menu shortcut, starts
  with Windows, daily exposure scan; full uninstaller that also
  removes every firewall rule it ever added.

### What VigilShield does NOT do (read this)

- It **cannot predict an attack before it starts**. Nobody's software
  can — not ours, not the billion-dollar security companies'. Anyone
  who promises that is lying.
- It is **not a replacement for antivirus**. Keep Windows Defender
  (free, built into Windows) or your antivirus turned on.
  VigilShield works *alongside* them as a second pair of eyes.
- It does **not stop 100% of attacks**. It blocks *known* threats
  fast and flags suspicious behaviour — that catches the common,
  everyday attacks, which is most of what hits home users.

### Who it's for

Everyday Windows users who want a simple, visible guard — people who
find big security suites confusing and just want to know: *is my PC
safe right now, and will something warn me if it's not?*

### Requirements

Windows 10 or 11. Run as administrator for auto-blocking
(without admin it still watches and warns). Internet needed for
threat-list updates.

### What you get

- `VigilShield.exe` — single file, no account, no subscription
- `install.bat` — one-click installer (Start Menu shortcut, auto-start
  with Windows, daily exposure scan) and full uninstaller
- This guide (plain-language setup, 5 minutes)
- Free updates to the program
